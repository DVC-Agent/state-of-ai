03 / Business Models

Price is not the business model.

Separate the billable unit, delivery responsibility and the cash required to grow.

Access

Subscription or seat. A predictable invoice can sit on top of variable serving costs.

Consumption

Usage or transaction. Cost follows activity, but the buyer still needs a link to value.

Work or outcome

Charge for completion. The vendor absorbs ambiguity, retries and failure cost.

Revenue → gross profit → contribution → cash

Inference, human review and support matter. No universal gross-margin or EBITDA band. Hybrid pricing can be a durable choice.
