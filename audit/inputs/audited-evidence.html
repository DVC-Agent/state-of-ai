SO WHAT?

The enduring advantage in agents will not come from having a model. It will come from orchestrating the full system around the model: memory, tools, workflows, reliability, and distribution. We have moved from prompt engineering to context engineering.

That architecture is the map of defensibility. The winners will not be the loudest at the frontier; they will be the ones who turn intelligence into dependable, repeatable execution.

If today's economics are strained, the next interface may rewrite them. The moment software starts transacting with software, the market changes shape again.

AGENTS TALKING TO AGENTS

We've built agents that talk to humans. The next frontier: agents that discover, hire, pay, and supervise each other.

October 16, 2026

Coordination is not the same as commerce

MCP connects agents to tools and data; A2A supports peer-to-peer agent collaboration. Discovery, identity, authorization, task handoff, settlement and dispute handling remain separate design responsibilities.

A payment rail does not remove principal accountability

Programmable balances and API payments can support machine-executed transactions. Someone still authorizes the agent, funds the account, defines spending limits and handles fraud, refunds, tax and disputes. Automated payment is not independent legal personhood.

FOUNDER TAKEAWAY

The agent-to-agent economy is real enough to invest in, but early enough that the biggest winners may not be the agents themselves — they may be the companies that provide the protocols, identity, payment rails, and trusted execution environments that let agents safely discover, hire, pay, and supervise one another.

That software stack still runs on steel, silicon, and electricity. The more capable AI becomes, the more brutally physical the system underneath it gets.

Outcomes are a commercial model, not a universal endpoint

Software suppliers and service operators are experimenting with work-unit and outcome contracts. The strongest fit is a result the provider controls and the customer can verify. AI is already reshaping how industries operate, compete, and ship product. Yet many of the companies building the core technology are still burning cash to deliver that transformation. The demand is undeniable; the economics are still unresolved.

October 16, 2026

Price the economic unit, not a fashionable label

Seats can monetize access and predictable workflows. Usage can align price with variable model costs. Outcome pricing can align the customer bill with value, but requires a measurable outcome, attribution, acceptable liability and a clear escalation policy. Hybrid contracts can combine all three. No revenue-growth comparison alone proves one pricing model is universally superior.

AI software does not automatically inherit traditional SaaS margins

Bessemer’s pricing research describes roughly 50–60% gross margins among the AI businesses it studied, versus the familiar 80–90% traditional SaaS reference.
